How AI Predicts the Next Word (Explain Like I'm 5)

1

✂️ Tokenize (Scissors)

The computer cuts sentences into individual word stickers (called tokens), stripping punctuation and ignoring capital letters.

2

📝 Tally Notebook (Bigrams)

Whenever Word A is followed by Word B, it adds a tally mark in its memory: "quick" → {"brown": 2, "rabbit": 1}.

3

🏆 Pick the Winner

When you enter a word, it opens that page and chooses the word with the most votes (or picks with weighted probability).

4

🔗 Build the Sentence

It repeats this step 5 times in a row, chaining predictions together to create a brand new sentence!

How Bigram Probability Works:

If "the" is followed by "lazy" 3 times and "quick" 2 times (5 total), then "lazy" has a 3 / 5 = 60% chance, and "quick" has a 2 / 5 = 40% chance.
